# dnkLauncher: a frontend core for the hybrid ports

Danik's idea, 2026-10-25; the decisions below are Danik's of the same day, from an interview, and the answers to its open questions given the same day. Nothing of it is built yet.

**An extra project, in alpha** (Danik, 2026-10-25: "for now, we will not break anything the users use"). Nothing players have changes for it: the launcher keeps every feature it has, its screen from *Scripts* included, and the READMEs, `docs/readme.md` and its section *The launcher* stay as they are. The frontend is not published (not in the database, not on the launcher's screen) until Danik says so.

## Script or core

The largest decision, researched before the interview (Main_MiSTer 8328d9f of 2026-10-25, our launcher's screen `launcher/ui/danik_hybrid_ports_ui.c`, the MiSTer forum):

| | Script (menu core, from *Scripts*) | Core (a hybrid core whose program is the frontend) |
|---|---|---|
| Reached from | the OSD's *Scripts* page only: an MGL loads a core, never a script; cannot be `bootcore` | a menu entry like any core, in the player's layout |
| 15kHz CRT | Main nags "modify MiSTer.ini" (`fb_terminal=2` or `vga_scaler=1`, `menu.cpp` `vga_nag()`) | `hybrid_host` video: 15kHz, interlace rules, *CRT Options*, *HDMI Only*, as every port |
| Picture | the menu core's, whatever the player's `video_mode` is | the purple screen and the ports' look |
| Input | the pad as keys (d-pad, A B X Y L R; no Start, Select or sticks) | the core's `J1` mapping, Menu OK / Back, the OSD hold |
| Sound | none | the core's (not used: below) |
| After a game | the MiSTer menu | the frontend again (below) |
| Cost | none to open | one FPGA load to open it (a second or two, not measured) and one more core to keep at the host version |

Decided: **a core**. A script's two worst points (no menu entry, the MiSTer.ini change on CRTs) cannot be fixed by its program. `load_core <file>.mgl` on `/dev/MiSTer_cmd` starts a game from either (`input.cpp`, MGL and MRA both taken).

## Decisions

- **Name**: the engine is `dnkLauncher` (`CONF_STR`, `games/dnk/dnkLauncher/`, `logs/dnk/dnkLauncher/`); the menu entry is **Hybrid Ports**: `_Other/dnk-Hybrid Ports.mgl`, or `Hybrid Ports.mgl` in the other layouts.
- **Scope**: the hybrid ports, every game of every port the database has, and the ScummVM games the player marked as favourites in ScummVM (*Systems*, below; Danik, 2026-10-29). The official consoles and computers, each a banner that starts its core (*Other cores*, below; Danik, 2026-10-31); no ROMs.
- **The launcher's screen as a page of the frontend too**: installing and removing ports and games, *Launcher* on / off, *Menu entries*, Apply. The launcher's own screen from *Scripts* (`danik_hybrid_ports.sh`, `danik_hybrid_ports_ui`, `dialog` over SSH) stays as it is, with every feature it has: the page is a second way to the same, never in its place (Danik, 2026-10-25). The daemon stays as it is: it is what runs a port's program when its core is loaded.
- **Reached by** its menu entry; **games started from it return to it** when they quit; **every game's own menu entry stays** next to it. No `bootcore`.
- **Video 640x480**, the mode of the core's own purple screen: interlaced at 15kHz, progressive on HDMI and at 31kHz (the rule "a 15kHz screen never gets more than 15kHz"). Interlaced at field rate (host version 18; Danik, 2026-10-27): a new frame at every field, 60 pictures a second on a CRT, always, no OSD option; on HDMI without *HDMI Only* what moves combs, and the README says to switch it on. Its one mode: the frontend is not a game, so the rule that a core starts at 320x200 does not apply to it (Danik, 2026-10-25). First 640x240; the mock-ups showed 240 lines too tight for the MiSTer logo, the badge and a proper font (Danik, the same day). No HUD, so no HUD lines; *HDMI Only* and *CRT Options* as in every core.
- **The look** (the mock-ups' second round, Danik: "it looks so much better"): the MiSTer logo at the top left and the danik badge at the bottom right, where the core's own screen has it; the purple as a gradient; panels with a rim, light from above and a shadow; the cursor a bar from the pink of "FPGA" to peach (square, from the panel's rim to the scroll bar's place, which it never goes under; on the list's first and last rows round where the panel's corners are, as the rim: Danik, 2026-10-27); the scroll bar at the panel's right within its rim, 4 pixels wide, from its top to its bottom, round at the corners as the panel is, gold stars, cyan for ports and years, coral for missing data.
- **Fonts, the player's choice in *Options***: Chakra Petch SemiBold the default, Nunito ExtraBold, Atkinson Hyperlegible Bold, Rubik Medium and Lexend SemiBold the others (Danik, 2026-10-25). All SIL OFL. Picked from 25 measured for interlace flicker (`tools/dnklauncher_fonts.py`): the smooth fonts flicker least (0.14 to 0.16; the 8x8 font of the hybrid core screens and Terminus 0.24 to 0.25), being lit in both fields at their soft edges.
- **Colour schemes, the player's choice in *Options*** (Danik, 2026-10-26: "I like all of the colour schemes, include all of them"): MiSTer Purple (the default), Midnight, Synthwave, Arcade, Amber Phosphor, Sunset, Ocean and Lavender (the one light scheme). A scheme sets every colour of the frontend: the ground's gradient, the panels and their rim, the text, the cursor's bar and the sort mode's pill, the accent (ports, versions), the favourites' stars and missing data. Each keeps its own bar ("the bar should remain the default colour for each colour scheme"): no separate bar setting. The core's own purple screen stays as it is; the start animation fades from it into the scheme's ground. `THEMES` of `tools/dnklauncher_mockup.py`.
- **A pattern over the ground, settings in *Options*** (Danik, 2026-10-26, picked on a page that tried them over the real screens): a checker of 32-pixel squares, 25% darker than the scheme's gradient, moving down and right 30 pixels a second (a pixel every second field). *Pattern* (Off, Checker, Diamonds, Stripes, Columns, Rows, Grid, Dots, Zigzag, Bricks; Off the gradient alone), *Pattern intensity* (5% to 50% darker), *Pattern strength* (even, or strongest at the top, the bottom or the edges, fading away from there), *Pattern direction* (eight) and *Pattern speed* (Still, Very slow, Slow, Normal, Fast, Very fast: a quarter to three pixels a frame). Every pixel is the gradient (A) or A darker (B), never a mix, and the pattern moves by whole pixels: the screen is put together over each once and a frame is copied from the two in runs, so the moving ground costs a copy of the frame, not the screen laid together again (on an interlaced screen the rows are copied from the two already through the flicker filter, but for those the pattern's edges cross). `GROUND` of `tools/dnklauncher_mockup.py`; the program's `src/ground.c`.
- **A flicker filter, a setting in *Options*** (Danik, 2026-10-25): a vertical [1 2 1] / 4 over the picture, only while 640x480 is interlaced (a 15kHz screen); it brings every font to 0.05 to 0.07. Off by default (Danik, 2026-10-27, with the pattern: the look he picked was without it). Light by default from 2026-10-28 (Danik: "default flicker filter = light for interlaced resolutions"), as the OSD's line in every core. From host version 18 the core filters (`FLICKER` 1 in `core/dnkLauncher.sv`; Danik, 2026-10-27): the same pixels, none of the ARM's time, which the Carousel needed (4.6 ms a frame on the ARM, 5 to 11 late frames of 1150 with it on); the program filters itself only with a core of an older host (`MH_HasFlickerFilter()` 0). Off, Light or Strong (Danik, 2026-10-28), as the OSD's *Flicker Filter* line every core with interlaced output has, and the two are kept in step: the row sets the line (`MH_SetFlickerFilter()`), the line sets the row (`MH_FlickerFilter()`), and the setting saved in `settings.txt` goes to the line at the start; on the ARM, Light is Strong.
- **Button symbols after the controller** (Danik, 2026-10-25): a Switch, Xbox or PlayStation controller is recognised by its USB vendor (Nintendo 057e, Microsoft 045e, Sony 054c; the library reads them already, `convention_codes()` of `hps/mister_joymap.c`) and the hints show its own buttons: Switch's letters and − / +, Xbox's coloured letters with View and Menu, PlayStation's shapes with Create and Options. Any other controller has MiSTer's own SNES names, unless the player picks a family in *Options* (*Button symbols*: Auto, Switch, Xbox, PlayStation, SNES). **The actions are by letter** (Danik, 2026-10-25): the same letter on every pad, Play A, Exit B, Favourite Y, Ports X (PlayStation: Cross, Circle, Triangle, Square, in Xbox's places). A Switch has Xbox's layout mirrored across the diagonal, so there all four are mirrored, Play on the right button as Menu OK is in the MiSTer menu for Nintendo's pads; not Play and Exit mirrored and the other two by place, as the first mock-ups had it. Play and Exit are Menu OK and Menu Back: with a mapping of its own in the MiSTer menu a controller has them where that says. A hint is drawn by the button's place (Linux names a pad's buttons so, `BTN_SOUTH` the bottom one). A controller of no known family has its places named as MiSTer names them (SNES: A east, B south, X north, Y west). With several controllers, the one pressed last.
- **A keyboard** (Danik, 2026-10-27): the arrow keys are the d-pad (held, they repeat as it does), Enter (and the keypad's) *Menu OK*, Esc *Menu Back*, on every screen. A letter on the game list puts the cursor on the first game, from the top in the sort shown, whose title starts with it, either case; without one the cursor stays. The core's own keyboard (`mh_input.keys`), held back while the OSD is open as every input.
- **A mouse** (Danik, 2026-10-27): a pointer while it moves, gone once the pad is used or after 5 seconds still; the arrow or the dart (*Pointer* in *Options*), both in the scheme's text colour with a rim against it (dark round a light fill, light round a dark one) and a soft shadow (the mock-ups' `POINTERS`; drafts in the bar's colours were not taken). A hint under it gets a chip's ground (`layer_hover()`). Left on a hint does what it says (Exit's question: its two), on a sort mode picks it, on a game puts the cursor there and a double click opens *Details*; in *Options* a click on a row puts the cursor there, on the cursor's row takes the next value, on a value's arrows the one before or after; on the ports page a click on a row puts the cursor there, on the cursor's row or a tickbox ticks it (never Apply but by its hint). The scroll bars are held and dragged. Right is Back everywhere, middle Favourite (the list and *Details*); the wheel moves the cursor, in *Details* to the game before or after. A pixel a count, as the launcher's own screen.
- **Options**, a page of the frontend (Start): the pages and their rows are in the section *Options* below (Danik, 2026-11-01). The font's name in the font itself, and under the rows a short box with one or two lines of the small font saying what the option under the cursor does: no heading, no examples (Danik, 2026-10-26: a horizontal split, not the list and a panel beside it). Seven rows in sight; a longer page scrolls, with a scroll bar as the game list's; kept in `games/dnk/dnkLauncher/settings.txt`.
- **No sound.** `MIDI_SYNTH` 0.
- **Its own start: no intro.** The core shows its screen as it is at the intro's end (the purple, the MiSTer logo in the middle, the badge) without the logo's 4.1 seconds, and the list comes as soon as its program is up, out of that picture (*Animations*, below).
- **Animations** (Danik, 2026-10-25: "flashy, elegant and fast"): pages, sort modes and the cursor move and fade instead of jumping. Below.
- **Layout: a list and a preview** (below), and **a page per game, *Details*** (Danik, 2026-10-25): the main screen shows the list and the artwork only, larger; A on a game opens its *Details* (the logo, the screenshot, every fact, the description), where A plays and B goes back. More room on the main screen, more to read on the game's page.
- **Sort modes, one list**: a button cycles A-Z, By port, Recent, Favourites. The mode is remembered.
- **Layouts of the game list** (Danik, 2026-10-27): *List* (the list and the preview, below) and *Carousel*; more may come. L2 / R2 on the main screen take the layout before and after, round, and *Options* has the line *Layout*; the layout is remembered. It changes the main screen alone: *Details*, *Options* and the *Repo* page are the same in every layout. The name of the layout comes a moment between the MiSTer logo and the title, with L2 and R2 beside it (`TOAST_FIELDS`, 1.5 s; the hints have no room for a fourth hint). L2 / R2 are the core's two buttons *Layout Previous* / *Layout Next* (`J1`), which MiSTer's default map gives no button, so the library also reads a gamepad's own (`MH_ReadTriggerButtons()`: `BTN_TL2` / `BTN_TR2`, or an analog trigger pressed past the middle; not a wheel's pedals). The keyboard has no key for it.
- **The Carousel** (Danik, 2026-10-27; its look picked on the tuning page, the Carousel's mock-up: https://claude.ai/artifact/LqVo9gdxYHkk9dCqByPzPH, "Cover flow" changed): the selected game's screenshot in the middle as it is (256x192, its top at y 160), with an outline in the bar's colours, floating 6 pixels up and down; four games each side, turned 60 degrees back and stacked (200 pixels to the first, 60 to each further one, 255 and 35 deeper), darker by 25% and 15% more each, the outermost fading in and out; every card drawn column by column in perspective, each pixel the nearest of its screenshot's (no filtering). Under each a reflection on the floor, 35% fading out over 45% of its height, mirrored about where the card stands, so it moves the other way as the card floats, drawn before the card (never over it) and fading out by y 400 (never cut off); the floor below the cards darker. Above it the game's logo (fitted into 300x46, `<id>.clogo`) and its year and developer, the count ("13 / 42") under the sort modes. Left / Right a game (12 fields a step, held: faster after 8), L / R five, Up / Down the first game of the next letter or of the one before, a letter its first game, round the ends; the logo crosses to the next game halfway through a step, sliding 40 pixels. The mouse: a card at a side goes to it, a double click on the middle one opens *Details*. Favourites, sort modes and *Details* as in the list: the carousel is the list's games, its cursor the list's.
- **The *Repo* page** (Danik, 2026-10-27; the ports page until then): the hint and the page that ticks ports and games and applies them are called *Repo* **Reached from every screen but *Options* and itself** (Danik, 2026-10-31: "It should only be missing in the options page and the repos page itself"): *Systems*, every System's list and every *Details*, and every System to come. It opens over the screen it was called from, whose panels sink and go as they do for *Options*, and Back returns there. After an Apply the list is read again: back to *Systems* if it came from there, else to the list it was over (the game of *Details* may be gone).
- **Per game**: its logo, **a gameplay screenshot of its own** (a new asset for every game, as the logos were), year and developer, a description, the port and its version.
- **Games whose data is missing** are listed, dimmed and marked; the preview says which files go where, the text of the core's own message.
- **A game started from the frontend starts shorter** (Danik, 2026-10-27; the player saw the MiSTer logo when the frontend started): Play's zoom into the screenshot and its fade to black as now, the black of the core's load, then the purple with the badge fading in, no intro and no MiSTer logo, and the data check's two lines, "Loading", the game's logo and the rest as now. Its own menu entry keeps the full start.

## Animations

A frame per field (59.64 a second), as `tools/dnklauncher_mockup.py` draws them (`anim_*`; animated WebP at the real speed and at a quarter). The program keeps each part of a screen as a layer of its own (the ground, the logo, the title, the sort modes, each panel with what is in it, the hints, the badge) and only puts them together, moved and faded, once per field: nothing is drawn anew while an animation runs but a row's text when the list changes. Curves: ease-out (cubic) for what arrives, ease-in-out for what leaves or crosses.

- **Start** (48 fields, 0.8 s): from the core's own screen, the frame its program takes over. The flat purple fades away from over the ground (the gradient and its pattern, already moving), the MiSTer logo goes from the middle to the top left at half its size, the badge from the core's place to the footer's, smaller; the title and the sort modes slide in, then the panels rise 18 pixels into place one after the other (the list, the preview, the hints).
- **The cursor** (7 fields per step): the bar glides to the next row, the rows under it inked as under the bar wherever it is; the list scrolls only when the bar comes within two rows of its edge. The preview crosses over to the next game, its logo sliding in 10 pixels. Held down, each step starts from where the last one is. *Options*' list the same (Danik, 2026-10-27: "the options menu should also have smooth scrolling"), what the option does changing at once. The bar is one shape wherever it is: the strip of rows under it has its colours top to bottom without a break, and the bar is cut out of it where it is (a bar a row showed the gap between two rows across it while it glided; Danik, 2026-10-27).
- **Sort mode** (Select, 14 fields): the pink pill slides to the next mode; the old list leaves 24 pixels to the left and fades, the new one comes from the right. The cursor stays on the same game.
- **A page** (*Options*, the ports page; 17 fields): the main screen's panels sink 12 pixels and fade one after the other, the page's rise 14 pixels; the System's title and count fade and the page's name comes in the title's place (*Options*; *Repo*, its count of ports beside it: Danik, 2026-11-02), the hints cross over. The logo and the badge stay. Back is the same, the other way.
- ***Details*** (A, 22 fields): the screenshot travels from the preview to its place in *Details* (the same size, so it only moves) and the logo to the top of the facts' panel, scaled; the list leaves 28 pixels to the left and fades, the sort modes go up and fade, and *Details*' panels come up around the screenshot, the facts after it has passed them, the description last. B plays it backwards.
- **Play** (A in *Details*, 38 fields): a pink glow round the screenshot flashes, the rest of the screen goes dark while the screenshot grows from its box to the whole screen, then the screen fades to black and the game's core is loaded. The game's own start comes out of black. The screenshot grows by whole pixels, each the nearest of its own, blocky (Danik, 2026-10-27: "I don't care if the pixels are blocky"): until then it was mixed (bilinear) in 32 bits and Details put together again every field, which was choppy on the MiSTer. Now Details is put together once, the screenshot's rows are picked from its 16-bit copy, and only what it does not cover goes dark.

- **The Carousel**: a step glides in 12 fields (ease-out), held each from where the last one is; the selected card floats all the time. The start, sort modes, pages and *Details* do with the cards what they do with the list's panel (the cards rise or slide and fade with it; *Details*' screenshot travels from the middle card). **A layout** (L2 / R2, 18 fields): the old one fades out sinking 12 pixels (in its first 11 fields), the new one comes up 14 pixels from the sixth; the sort modes, the hints and the head stay.

**Never in the way**: a button pressed while an animation runs takes it to its end at once and does what it does (Play during the start plays). Moving the cursor never waits for the preview. Whatever does not fit a field (a screen of rows' text) is drawn ahead, in the field the cursor stops.

## Where everything comes from

- **The list**: the launcher's catalogue (`Scripts/.danik_hybrid_ports/catalogue.txt`, every published port and game) for the ports page; for the game list, the games installed (`games/dnk/<Engine>/games.txt` there).
- **The pictures are the frontend's own**, made once and never scaled on the MiSTer: `tools/dnklauncher_art.py` makes them from every published port's repository, and the frontend brings them in `games/dnk/dnkLauncher/art/`: per game `<Engine>/<id>.shot` (the screenshot, 4:3 at 256x192: the preview and *Details* draw it the same size), `<id>.logo` (fitted into the preview's 244x64), `<id>.dlogo` (into *Details*' 252x77) and `<id>.plogo` (into the ports page's 178x80), and `index.txt` with each game's title, year, developer and description. Scaled in linear light with Lanczos, dithered to RGB565 (4x4 ordered), in a file the program reads as it is ("DNKI", the size, RGB565, an alpha byte a pixel for the logos; the tool's header). 7.1 MB for the 42 games of the 12 published ports (2026-10-27). Once the frontend is published, `distribution.py` runs it at every publish (as it makes the catalogue) and puts the tree in the database as launcher files (tag `launcher`), so every player who has the launcher has them, an already published port shows its art without being packaged again, and the ports page can show ports that are not installed. Not before: while the frontend is in alpha nothing of it goes into the database, which every player's launcher installs from.
- **New assets in each core's repository**, written and taken by Claude, quality-checked by Danik (2026-10-25): descriptions in Claude's own words (others found online may inspire them, never copied), screenshots from the running games (`fakecore` on the PC, as TheForceEngine's README shot; `MISTER_HYBRID_FPS=0`), each gameplay that looks played: a moment of a level, the HUD's normal values, no cheat's traces, no message on the screen. The files: `package/screens/<id>.png` for every game of `games.txt` (gameplay, never a menu: looked at before it goes in; the picture as `fakecore`'s `shot` saves it, the size of the mode it was taken in, not stretched: the core's default mode, a 320-wide one where the game has it, which `distribution.py` scales to 4:3; a game's own level, an expansion's from the expansion, not the place the README's screenshots show), and `package/info.txt`, a line per game, `<id>|<year>|<developer>|<description>`, the description at most 4 lines of *Details* (about 340 characters). `games.txt` stays as it is (`MH_PickGame()`, `distribution.py` and older launchers read it). Required for every game, as the logos are: `distribution.py` refuses a port without them once the frontend is out, and a game without them meanwhile has the port's `docs/screenshot2.png` and no text.
- **Data missing**: today a game's files are known only to its port's C code (`MH_Game.files`, jfSW checks by checksum, `MH_NOT_THERE`). Decided (Danik, 2026-10-25): a mode of the library, `<program> --check`, which does `MH_PickGame()`'s check without the core and prints for each game whether it is there, and its message if not. The frontend runs it for every port in the background once the list is up and keeps the result until the game's folder changes (its time). A port built before it has no marks. Not the other way, the patterns written in `info.txt` too: the same thing in two places.
- **Recent**: the daemon writes every game it starts, from the frontend or its own entry, into `games/dnk/dnkLauncher/recent.txt` (set name and time; a launcher change, its `VERSION`). **Favourites** and the sort mode: `games/dnk/dnkLauncher/settings.txt`, the frontend's.

## Starting a game and coming back

1. The frontend writes `/tmp/dnk_launched` (the set name) and sends `load_core <the game's menu entry>` to `/dev/MiSTer_cmd`: the MGL of the player's layout, so the set name and the OSD settings are the game's.
2. **Shorter start** (Danik, 2026-10-27): the frontend's Play as now (the blocky zoom, then black), so the black while Main_MiSTer loads the core (the FPGA reprogrammed: no signal, which nothing can avoid) follows black and is no seam. Before `load_core` the frontend also writes a mark into the control block's memory in the DDR3, which a core's load leaves as it is; the new core's RTL reads it in its first vblank and starts without the intro: from black, the purple and the badge fading in, no MiSTer logo, as at the intro's end without it. The game's program takes the mark away at `MH_Open()` (and the daemon when the core changes to one that is not the game's), so that a load that failed leaves no core starting short from the MiSTer menu. The rest is the start every port has, without waiting for an intro: "Checking Game Data..." and "Game Data OK" as always, in their place under where the MiSTer logo would be (Danik, 2026-10-27: "the messages should appear like normal before the logo shows up"), or the message naming missing files; then "Loading <title>...", the game's logo fading in, its 2 seconds and the fade-out. About 5.5 seconds from the load to the first frame instead of 9.1, the load's black not measured yet (longer on a TV that syncs again over HDMI).
3. **Back**: a game that quits loads the frontend's menu entry instead of `menu.rbf` while the file names its set (`hps/mister_hybrid.c`, `load_core /media/fat/menu.rbf`), and removes the file. Quitting a game picked from a core's own list still brings that list back (exit code 42). Leaving the core from the OSD, or loading another, removes the file too (the daemon, when the core changes to one that is not the game's).
4. Both are a framework change, host version 18: a port has them once it is built again. Until then a game started from the frontend starts in full and quits to the MiSTer menu.

- **Another page**: its rows and help fade in over 11 fields (180 ms), the panels staying, where the workbench faded them too. A value's change, a move within a page, or going in and out of the rows does not fade.
- **About**, the last row of Library (the workbench's choice; the words Claude's, for Danik to change): over the rows and the help box, the rail staying: the name, "The frontend of the hybrid ports", the framework's version (the core's: `badge_version()`), by Danik with the Patreon, MiSTer Frontier's credit, the fonts' licence and the system logos' (es-theme-carbon, CC BY-NC-SA 2.0, as *Other cores* requires).
- **The mouse**: a page on the rail opens it, a row as before (on the cursor's row the next value, About opens), a switch flips, a grey row does nothing; the right button goes back a level as Back does.

## Game lists

Danik's request of 2026-11-02 ("How would you approach adding gamelists for all consoles? Would you take the gamelist.xml way other frontends take?"), decided the same day on the picks page (https://claude.ai/artifact/PzZoXFJAp9o3RYJSEDY1nF, its `picks` collection). The facts per system are gathered and the look approved (2026-11-02, below); in the program since the same day: step 1, the lists, and step 2, the screens (the list with the core's row, *Details*, the banner's count, the Options page, the Carousel), step 3, the art (fetched, baked and shown), and step 4, starting a game; next the PC demo.

**Not gamelist.xml.** EmulationStation's file is what a scraper writes; a MiSTer player has folders of games and none. What is in the player's folders is the truth, kept in lists of our own; a game's art is baked once into our sizes, as the ports' covers are, the program never decoding a scraper's PNG or JPEG while it draws. A gamelist.xml may be read later, for descriptions, years and makers (Danik: not for now).

- **Which**: every console of `assets/systems/systems.txt` (56). Computers later: disks, tapes and hard disk images, several files to one game, the art patchy.
- **Opening**: A on a console's banner opens its list; its first row is the core itself, which starts it with nothing loaded, on top under every sort mode, as ScummVM's launcher row is. A console with no games found opens the core's *Details*, as today.
- **Where**: the folder the MiSTer's own file browser opens for the core, `games/<folder>/` and its subfolders, on the SD card and on USB drives; the file types the core takes; **inside zips too**, each game in a zip a row, as the MiSTer shows them.
- **Looking for games**: only when the player asks, Options' *Look for games* (Danik's pick, over looking at every start), in a thread of its own on CPU0 at `SCHED_IDLE`, as the Carousel's faces are made. The lists are kept in `games/dnk/dnkLauncher/lists/<key>.txt` (and `scan.txt`, when it last ran and the games it found). Every folder is read again at each look (a folder's time cannot be trusted on a card written by another computer, and reading a folder is cheap); a zip's files are read again only when its size or time changed (to the nanosecond), its lines taken from the list before otherwise. Never while a frame waits. Built (2026-11-02, step 1 of the program): `src/lists.c` (dnkLauncher).
- **Names**: from the file's No-Intro name (GoodTools' too: `(U)`, `(JU)`, `[!]`, a bad dump, hack, trainer, overdump or translation in brackets an extra). An Options row, *Game names*: *Title alone* (the default: `Super Metroid`), *Title and region*, *File name* (Danik: "All these as options").
- **Regions and revisions**: one row per game, the version chosen in *Details* (the control a core's *Details* has for its several cores). Which comes first, for the row's art and A on the row: an Options row, *First region*, USA by default.
- **Hacks, betas, prototypes and demos** (No-Intro's `(Beta)`, `(Proto)`, `(Hack)`, `(Demo)`) are hidden, an Options row shows them; BIOS files never.
- **Art: libretro-thumbnails**, RetroArch's collection: `thumbnails.libretro.com/<system>/Named_Boxarts|Named_Logos|Named_Snaps|Named_Titles/<No-Intro name>.png`, the system's folder named as the No-Intro and Redump sets name it, `` & * / : ` < > ? \ | " `` in a name as `_`, PNG alone (checked 2026-11-02). All four: the box for *Game art*'s *Cover*, the logo and the screenshot for *Logo + Screenshot*, the title screen where a game has no screenshot. **Downloaded from Options** (*Download game art*), for every system with a list, the launcher usable meanwhile; run again, only what is new or missing. Baked into our sizes and kept in `games/dnk/dnkLauncher/` on the player's card. **Never shipped**: the project gives no licence, the art being its developers' and publishers'; nothing of it goes into a zip or the database. A game with no picture: a card of its name on the system's colours.
  - **Built** (2026-11-02, step 3; dnkLauncher's `src/art.c`): a thread at `SCHED_IDLE` on CPU0, as *Look for games*, from a copy of the lists taken when it starts. For each console it reads what the server has, its directory listings (`https://thumbnails.libretro.com/<system>/<Named_*>/`, about 80 KB compressed for the SNES's boxes), only those its games need and once a run; a file's picture is the listing's same name, else the same title of the same region (No-Intro's parts, `nointro_parse()`), else, but for a box, the same title. A box for each version (they differ by region), a snap and a logo for the game (the first version that has one), the title screen only where no snap was found. `curl` (24 files a run, one connection), each file decoded with stb_image (nothings/stb v2.30, public domain or MIT, PNG alone, 8192 pixels a side at most), scaled as `tools/dnklauncher_art.py` scales (Lanczos in linear light, the colour weighted by the alpha), dithered to `DNKI` the same way, and let go: `<DNK_HOME>/lists/art/<key>/<kind>/<name>.dnki`, `<name>` the file's with `` & * / : ` < > ? \ | " `` as `_`, the kinds `box` (267 high, its own width, 400 at most: the Carousel's), `pbox` (the pane's, 244 x 271, where the box is wider than 244), `dbox` (*Details*', within 256 x 192), `snap` and `title` (256 x 192, a handheld's its own shape within it), `logo` (244 x 64), `dlogo` (252 x 77), `clogo` (300 x 46). Many of the collection's files are git links (their text the name of the one they stand for, `California Games (World).png`): followed, twice at most. Again: only what is not there. The certificate is always checked (the system's, then MiSTer's own `/etc/ssl/certs/cacert.pem`), never fetched without: a picture is decoded on the MiSTer; no connection at all ends the run (*could not connect*). About 490 KB a game on the card (53 games of the test lists, 26 MB). The screens draw them as they are (the pane's box scaled to fill it, a few pixels, or smaller with a margin); the Carousel's faces of a console's game with art are made by its thread, as the ports' are; a game whose picture came is made again (the pane, *Details*, its face), at most every second while the download runs. A Carousel card with a screenshot and no logo has its name above it (a title card says it itself). `dnkLauncher --fetch-art` from the shell; `DNK_ART_URL` another server (a test's, in the same layout).
- **The lists**: both layouts, List and Carousel (the Carousel making its cards ahead near the cursor alone); sort modes *A to Z*, *Recent* and *Favourites*; *Details* without a gamelist.xml: the title, region, languages, revision, the file and its folder, the size, the system, *Last played*.
- **Starting a game**: an MGL written to `/tmp` (the core, then the file with its type, slot and delay), loaded as a core is (`launch_core()`); back to the launcher as from a core (`--watch`). Every system with a list is tried once on the MiSTer before it is called working.
  - **Built** (2026-11-02, step 4; dnkLauncher's `launch_console()`): `/tmp/dnk_console.mgl`, `<rbf>` the core's folder and name without the date (`_Console/SNES`), or a menu entry's own `<rbf>` and `<setname>` copied (`GameboyColor.mgl`: the Game Boy core as `GBC`); `<file>` the row's `delay`, `type` and `index`, its `path` **the whole path** where the list found it (a file in a zip `.../Set.zip/Pack/Game.md`, a Neo Geo romset its folder or zip), not `root="games"`: Main_MiSTer's `findPrefixDir()` takes the first drive that has the folder, `usb0` before the SD card, which loads another copy (or none) of a game found on the SD card when a drive has the folder too (Main_MiSTer's `menu.cpp` takes a `path` that starts with `/` as it is; `sxmlc` decodes `&amp;` in it). The core is the one *Details* shows, the row of its folder for it if there is one (the Lynx's two cores, each its own index). The core's row starts the core alone. The next start opens the list (`/tmp/dnk_from`), the cursor on the game played last.
- **The facts** (2026-11-02): dnkLauncher's `assets/systems/lists.txt`, a file of their own beside `systems.txt` (a console can have several rows: a CD folder, file types with other art, a variant with another core or slot), one row per folder, file types and MGL line: `key|variant|folder|types|mgl|delay|art`. `tools/systems_lists.py` checks them against each core's `CONF_STR` (the repositories of the MiSTer wiki's core list, from which the official database is made), the database's folders and menu entries, libretro-thumbnails' systems and Zaparoo's table (`mister/catalog/catalog.json` of zaparoo-core, GPL-3.0: read as data, nothing of its code taken; where it differs it is printed, not an error). 67 rows for 55 consoles; the AY-3-8500 has no list (its games are on the chip). What was found:
  - **The MGL's index** (Main_MiSTer's `menu.cpp`, read 2026-11-02): the OSD's `F` or `S` line whose one digit after `F`, `FS`, `FC` or `S` is the index (none written: 0), **the last** that has it; an index no line has leaves the MGL item's line at 0, the OSD's first line. The core itself gets another number for a line without a digit (its place in the menu plus one), so the two must not be confused. Zaparoo's indices are often one off (1 for NES's `FS,NES…`) and work only through that fallback. **ColecoVision**: its cartridge line and its SG-1000 line both read 0, so index 0 reaches *Load SG-1000* and the core takes a ColecoVision game for an SG-1000 one; its cartridges take an index no line has (9), its `.sg` files 0.
  - **Folders**: a core's file browser opens `games/<its CONF_STR name>` or its menu entry's setname (`Atari2600`, `GBC`, `GameGear`, `MegaDuck`, `PocketChallengeV2`, `WonderSwanColor`, the database's six console MGLs); a TurboGrafx-16 CD `TGFX16-CD` and a Neo Geo CD `NeoGeo-CD` (Main_MiSTer's `PCECD_DIR`, `NEOCD_DIR`). A two-player core opens a folder of its own (`GBA2P`); the list is the system's folder, and its games start with either core, the MGL giving the whole path.
  - **Art**: 43 of the 55 have a libretro-thumbnails system; Astrocade, BBC Bridge Companion, Gamate, Game & Watch (its `Handheld Electronic Game` is named after MAME, not the core's files), GameKing, FM Towns Marty, Mega Duck, My Vision, PocketStation, Pocket Challenge V2, Super Vision 8000 and VC 4000 have none: their games get the title card. A file type can take another system's art (Game Boy Color in the Game Boy's folder, SG-1000 in the Master System's and ColecoVision's, Famicom Disk System in the NES's, SuperGrafx and CDs in the TurboGrafx-16's).
  - **Neo Geo**: its games are romsets (a folder or a zip, `*`), named by the core's own list, not by No-Intro; its art (`SNK - Neo Geo`) by those names; how the names are read is for the program's step.
  - **Delay**: 1 second, 2 where Zaparoo's table found a core needs it (NES, the Game Boys, GBA, NGPC, SNES, Saturn, Mega Duck); none tried on the MiSTer yet. Lines a core can hide (`H`: the Master System's and Game Gear's, the PlayStation's CD) are to be tried there too.
- **The look** (2026-11-02, approved by Danik in two rounds on the review page https://claude.ai/artifact/FEJ2NW5eTua4j7kBzGkid4, its `picks` collection; the mock-ups' `--consoles`, `console_mockups()`):
  - **The list** as the ports' (the pane after *Game art*): under *Cover* the box at the pane's size in its own shape (a US Super Nintendo box wide, a Mega Drive box tall); under *Logo + Screenshot* the logo over the screenshot, a TV console's 4:3, a handheld's its screen's shape (the Game Boy's 10:9). The core's row reads **`<title> core`** (`Super Nintendo core`, the title of `systems.txt`), the pane the system's card. *Recent* gives the day played at a row's right (*today*, *yesterday*, *3 days ago*, *last week*).
  - **No art**: the title card, the system's two colours, the name, the system's logo faint below: 4:3 under *Logo + Screenshot*, 2:3 under *Cover*.
  - **Systems**: a console with a list has its count on the open banner's second line after the year and the type, `1990  Console  17 games` (games, not files: one per row of its list).
  - **The Carousel**: box art at **one height**, each box its own width (Danik's pick of three: one 2:3 card, one width); *Logo + Screenshot* cards as the ports'.
  - ***Details***: the picture after *Game art* (Danik's pick): under *Cover* the box at the picture's height (192), its own width, a 2:3 title card without one; else the screenshot, the title screen without one. The ports' *Details* differ there: their cover crosses over to the screenshot (Danik saw it said on the page). Right of it the logo (the name without one), the rule and the facts: *Region* (with several versions, Left and Right change it, `1 of 2` after it), *Languages* (`not named` where the name gives none), *Revision* (`first release`), *Last played*; below, one under the other, *File*, *Folder*, *Size*, *Core*.
  - **Built** (2026-11-02, step 2; dnkLauncher's `CLAUDE.md`): a console's games are a System of their own while its list is open (A on its banner; a console without a list keeps its *Details*), its title the console's. A list of thousands is drawn from a window of its rows (128, made again near its edges; the cursor's glide in it 24 rows at most), and the Carousel keeps its cards only near its place (24 each side), so that memory stays as for the ports. Until the art: every game the title card, made by the main thread as it comes in sight.
  - **Options**: a page of its own, ***Game lists***, before *Library* (a cartridge icon): *Game names*, *First region*, *Show betas and hacks* (a switch), *Look for games* and *Download game art*. The last two show what they did last (`3 days ago: 1,204 games`, `1,187 of 1,204 have art`), and while they run how far they are, with a bar 2 pixels high in the row (at its y + 25, under the text).

Not asked and decided unless Danik says otherwise: favourites, hidden games and *Last played* by system and title (a game's versions share them: a version is a choice in *Details*, not a game; `lists/marks.txt` and `played.txt`); Hybrid Ports and ScummVM as they are; Repo the hybrid ports' alone; Arcade out; alpha, as everything here.
